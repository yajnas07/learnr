🧠 Overview — Control & Coordination
⚡ Nervous System
Medium: Electrical impulse via neurons
Speed: Very fast (milliseconds)
Duration: Short-lived
Scope: Specific — through nerves
Components: CNS + PNS
Response: Immediate, precise
💊 Endocrine System
Medium: Chemical hormones via blood
Speed: Slow (seconds to minutes)
Duration: Long-lasting
Scope: Broad — via bloodstream
Components: Glands + hormones
Response: Diffuse, sustained
🔬 Neuron — Structural Unit
PartFunction

Cell Body (Cyton)Nucleus + organelles; metabolic centre

⚡ Reflex Action & Reflex Arc

Rapid, involuntary response to stimulus. Signal does NOT go to brain — processed in spinal cord. Brain informed after response.

TestesScrotum Testosterone Male secondary sexual characters, sperm production Deficiency → delayed puberty, reduced fertility

Negative Feedback: Blood sugar ↑ → Insulin released → Glucose stored → Blood sugar ↓ → Insulin stops. Maintains homeostasis.

🌱 Tropisms
☀️
Phototropism
Stimulus: Light
Shoot: +ve (toward)
Root: −ve (away)
Auxin → shaded side
⬇️
Geotropism
Stimulus: Gravity
Root: +ve (downward)
Shoot: −ve (upward)
💧
Hydrotropism
Stimulus: Water
Root: +ve (toward)
Helps roots find water
🌀
Thigmotropism
Stimulus: Touch
Tendrils coil around support
e.g. Pea, cucumber
Phototropism mechanism: Light → Auxin migrates to shaded side → More cell elongation → Shoot bends toward light. Darwin (1880) → Went (1926, named auxin).
Nastic movements (non-directional): Mimosa pudica — leaves fold on touch. Venus flytrap — snaps shut. Not tropism — no growth involved.
💡 Memory Tricks & Quick Facts
DAMN Mnemonic
Dendrites — Axon — Myelin — Nodes. The four structures of a neuron in signal order.
Brain Divisions
Fore = Think/Feel (Cerebrum, Hypothalamus, Thalamus)
Hind = Move/Live (Cerebellum, Medulla, Pons)
Reflex vs Voluntary
Reflex → Spinal cord (fast). Voluntary → Brain (slow). You feel pain after withdrawing hand.
Plant Hormones AGCA
Auxin, Gibberellin, Cytokinin = PROMOTERS. Abscisic Acid = INHIBITOR.
Endocrine Memory
Pituitary = MASTER (controls all). Adrenal = EMERGENCY. Thyroid = METABOLISM. Pancreas = SUGAR.
Medulla = Vital
Controls heartbeat, breathing, BP. Even slight damage → death. Never confuse with adrenal medulla (produces adrenaline).
⭐ High-Yield CBSE Points
  • Neuron is the structural and functional unit of the nervous system.
  • Myelin sheath speeds up nerve impulse by saltatory (jumping) conduction at Nodes of Ranvier.
  • Reflex arc does not involve the brain — processed at spinal cord. Brain informed after.
  • Cerebrum = largest brain part (~85%). Controls all voluntary and conscious activities.
  • Medulla oblongata = controls ALL involuntary vital functions. Critical for survival.
  • Hypothalamus = thermoregulation centre AND controls the pituitary gland (links NS + endocrine).
  • Pituitary = master gland. All other endocrine glands are under its control.
  • Adrenaline = "hormone of emergency". Prepares body for fight-or-flight.
  • Insulin deficiency → Diabetes mellitus. Insulin lowers blood sugar; glucagon raises it.
  • Thyroxine requires iodine. Deficiency → Goitre (swollen neck).
  • Auxin causes phototropism — migrates to shaded side, causes elongation → bending toward light.
  • Abscisic Acid (ABA) = only inhibitory plant hormone. Causes stomatal closure during drought.
  • Plants do NOT have nervous system — coordination only through phytohormones.
  • Negative feedback maintains hormonal homeostasis — rising hormone level suppresses further secretion.
  • Conditioned reflex (Pavlov) = learned reflex. Example: salivation at bell sound.
