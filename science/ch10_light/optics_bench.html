Sign Convention (New Cartesian): All distances measured from the pole/optical centre. Distances in the direction of incident light are positive; against incident light are negative. For mirrors: u is always −ve (object in front). v is −ve for real image, +ve for virtual. For lenses: u is always −ve. v is +ve for real image, −ve for virtual. Focal length: concave mirror & convex lens = negative f (mirror) / positive f (lens).
