Lifelines of National Economy

Roadways, railways, pipelines, waterways, airways, communication and international trade

🛣️ Road Transport
India has one of the largest road networks in the world (~62.16 lakh km). Roads are cheaper to build, suit hilly & dissected terrain, and offer door-to-door service. They carry ~85% of passenger and ~70% of freight traffic.

Classification of Roads

TypeDescription
Golden Quadrilateral6-lane super highways linking Delhi–Mumbai–Chennai–Kolkata; N-S & E-W corridors. By NHAI.
National HighwaysLink states & major cities; maintained by CPWD. E.g., NH-1 (Delhi–Amritsar).
State HighwaysLink state capital with district headquarters; built by State PWD.
District RoadsConnect district headquarters with other places of the district.
Border RoadsStrategic roads in border areas; built by Border Roads Organisation (BRO).
Road density = length of road per 100 sq km of area. It is highest in Kerala and lowest in Jammu & Kashmir.
🚆 Rail Transport
Railways are the principal mode of transport for freight and passengers, and the largest public sector undertaking in India. The first train ran in 1853 from Mumbai to Thane (34 km).

Railway Gauges

🛤️

Broad Gauge

Distance between rails = 1.676 m. Most common; "Project Unigauge" converts other lines to broad gauge.

🛤️

Metre Gauge

Distance = 1.0 m. Being converted to broad gauge for uniformity.

🛤️

Narrow Gauge

0.762 m & 0.610 m — mostly in hilly areas (e.g., Nilgiri, Darjeeling toy trains).

🌊

Konkan Railway

Opened 1998; links Mumbai to Mangaluru along the west coast — an engineering marvel with many tunnels.

Factors Affecting Distribution

Railway distribution depends on relief, population and economic activity. It is dense in the northern plains (level land, dense population, farming) but sparse in the Himalayas, deserts, forests and the marshy Sundarbans.
🛢️ Pipelines & Waterways
Pipelines transport crude oil, petroleum products and natural gas. Waterways are the cheapest, most fuel-efficient and eco-friendly means for carrying heavy and bulky goods.

✈️ Air Transport
Air transport is the fastest, most comfortable and prestigious mode. It can cover difficult terrain — high mountains, deserts and oceans — with ease. Air travel was nationalised in 1953.
🌐

Air India

Provides international air services connecting India to the rest of the world.

🛫

Domestic Services

Indian Airlines & Alliance Air (plus private airlines) provide domestic connectivity.

🚁

Pawan Hans

Provides helicopter services to the petroleum sector, NE region and inaccessible/hilly areas.

🆘

Vital in Emergencies

Crucial for the North-East (difficult relief) and for disaster relief operations.

Main international airports: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru & Hyderabad. Airports are managed by the Airports Authority of India (AAI).
📡 Communication Networks
Communication helps share information over long distances. It is of two types — personal (post, telephone) and mass communication (radio, TV, newspapers, films).

Personal Communication

📮

Postal Network

India has the largest postal network in the world. 1st class (cards, envelopes) & 2nd class (book packets, registered). Rapid/Speed/Media post.

📞

Telecom

One of the largest telecom networks in Asia. Uniform STD rates across India via satellite. 24-hour STD everywhere.

Mass Communication

📻

Radio (Akashvani)

All India Radio broadcasts since 1923 — programmes on information, education and entertainment.

📺

Doordarshan

One of the largest terrestrial television networks in the world.

📰

Press & Films

Newspapers in ~100+ languages; India is the largest producer of feature films in the world.

🌍 International Trade & Tourism
Trade is the exchange of goods and services. International trade — between countries via sea, air or land — is the economic barometer of a nation.

Balance of Trade

Balance of trade = Exports − Imports. It is favourable when exports exceed imports, and unfavourable when imports exceed exports.
