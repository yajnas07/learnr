Lifelines of National Economy · Geography Ch.7 · Quick Reference

Roadways

  • India has one of the largest road networks in the world (~62 lakh km)
  • Cheaper to build; suit hilly terrain; door-to-door service
  • Carry ~85% passengers & ~70% freight traffic
  • Golden Quadrilateral — Delhi-Mumbai-Chennai-Kolkata (NHAI)
  • Types: National, State, District, Border roads
Road density highest in Kerala, lowest in Jammu & Kashmir. Border Roads by BRO.

Railways

  • Principal mode of transport; largest public sector undertaking
  • First train: 1853, Mumbai to Thane (34 km)
  • Gauges: Broad (1.676 m), Metre (1.0 m), Narrow (0.762/0.610 m)
  • Project Unigauge — convert all to broad gauge
  • Dense in northern plains; sparse in hills/deserts/Sundarbans
Konkan Railway (1998) links Mumbai–Mangaluru along the west coast.

Airways

  • Fastest, most comfortable & prestigious mode
  • Covers difficult terrain — mountains, deserts, oceans
  • Nationalised in 1953
  • Air India — international; Indian Airlines — domestic
  • Pawan Hans — helicopter services to oil sector & NE region
Airports managed by Airports Authority of India (AAI).

Communication

  • Personal (post, telephone) & mass (radio, TV, press, films)
  • India has the largest postal network in the world
  • Post: 1st class (cards/envelopes) & 2nd class (book packets)
  • Radio Akashvani (1923); TV Doordarshan
  • India = largest producer of feature films in the world
Uniform STD rates across India via satellite; 24-hour STD everywhere.

International Trade

  • Trade = exchange of goods & services
  • International trade = economic barometer of a nation
  • Balance of trade = Exports − Imports
  • Favourable: exports > imports · Unfavourable: imports > exports
  • Exports: gems, chemicals, textiles, engineering goods
Imports: petroleum, machinery, gold, electronics.

Tourism

  • A growing industry supporting many others
  • Over 15 million people employed directly
  • Promotes national integration & foreign exchange
  • Supports local handicrafts & culture
  • Types: heritage, eco, medical, adventure, business
Foreign tourists visit for heritage, beaches, wildlife & medical care.
