Power Sharing

Class X · Civics Ch.1 · Quick Revision Cheat Sheet — 12 Panels

🇧🇪 Belgium — Accommodation
Ethnic composition: 59% Dutch (Flemish, north), 40% French (Walloon, south), 1% German (east)
Brussels (capital): 80% French + 20% Dutch — in a Dutch-speaking region — creating tension
Solution: 4 constitutional amendments (1970–1993) transforming Belgium into a federal state
Central cabinet: equal Dutch & French ministers by constitutional law
3 levels: Federal + Regional (Flanders/Wallonia/Brussels) + Community Govts (cultural/education)
Result: Peaceful coexistence — Belgium is headquarters of the EU today
🇱🇰 Sri Lanka — Majoritarianism
Ethnic composition: Sinhala 74%, Sri Lankan Tamil 11%, Indian Tamil 7%, Moors 7%
1956: Sinhala Only Act — declared Sinhala the only official language; Tamil excluded
University quotas & government jobs heavily favoured Sinhala; Buddhism made state religion
1976: LTTE founded — Liberation Tigers of Tamil Eelam; demanded independent "Eelam"
Civil war 1983–2009 — 26 years, 70,000–100,000 dead, millions displaced
Lesson: Majoritarianism alienates minorities and ultimately destroys the nation

💡 Why Share Power?
🧠 Prudential Reasons (practical): Sharing power reduces conflict and ensures political stability. Civil wars are costly for everyone — even the majority loses. Social peace enables economic development.
⚖️ Moral Reasons (principled): Democracy = rule by ALL the people, not just 50%+1. Every citizen has a right to participate. Majoritarianism is tyranny — anti-democratic in spirit.
Power sharing is the very spirit of democracy — it respects the dignity of ALL citizens
🏛️ Form 1 — Horizontal (Organs of Govt)
Power shared among Legislature, Executive, Judiciary — the 3 organs — at the same level
Each organ can check and balance the others — no single organ can dominate
India examples: Judiciary can strike down unconstitutional laws (judicial review) · President cannot make laws · Parliament cannot deliver verdicts
Also called Separation of Powers
🗺️ Form 2 — Vertical (Levels of Govt)
Power divided among Central, State, and Local governments — federalism
LevelIndia Example
CentralUnion List — defence, foreign affairs, currency (97 subjects)
StateState List — police, agriculture, health (66 subjects)
BothConcurrent List — education, forests, trade unions (47 subjects)
LocalPanchayats & Municipalities (73rd & 74th Amendments, 1992)
🫂 Form 3 — Social Groups
Power shared with religious, linguistic, and other minority communities
Prevents social exclusion and ensures marginalised groups have a voice in governance
India: Reserved seats for SC/ST in Parliament & State Assemblies · 1/3 reservation for women in Panchayats · Minority Commission · Right to run own educational institutions
Belgium: Equal French & Dutch representation in cabinet  |  Lebanon: President = Christian, PM = Sunni, Speaker = Shia
🗳️ Form 4 — Political Parties & Movements
Multi-party system prevents monopoly — parties must compete and negotiate
Coalition government: two or more parties share power — example: most Indian govts since 1989 (NDA, UPA)
Pressure groups — trade unions, farmers' bodies, student unions — influence govt decisions
Civil society & free media — keep government accountable; 4th estate
Even within parties, different factions ensure power is not concentrated in one person
📖 Key Definitions
Majoritarianism: majority community rules however it wants, ignoring minority wishes
Ethnic group: social group with common cultural tradition, language, history, or religion
Civil war: armed internal conflict between govt and a section of its own people
Coalition govt: government formed by 2+ parties agreeing to cooperate and share power
Federalism: power divided between central authority and constituent state/regional units
Decentralisation: transfer of power from central/state to local bodies (Panchayats, Municipalities)
📋 Four Forms — Quick Summary
FORM 1 · HORIZONTAL
Among organs of govt Legislature + Executive + Judiciary · Checks & Balances
FORM 2 · VERTICAL
Among levels of govt Central + State + Local · Federalism + Decentralisation
FORM 3 · SOCIAL GROUPS
Among communities Reservations + Minority rights + Community representation
FORM 4 · POLITICAL PARTIES
Among parties & movements Multi-party + Coalition + Pressure groups + Media
