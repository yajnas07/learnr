Water Resources · Geography Ch.3 · Quick Reference

Water — Key Facts

  • Water is a renewable resource (hydrological cycle)
  • 96.5% of world water is in oceans (saline)
  • Only 2.5% is freshwater — most frozen in glaciers
  • Groundwater = 0.68% | Rivers/lakes/air = 0.07%
  • India: ~4% of world rainfall, ~2.5% of water, ~18% population
  • India's rainfall is highly seasonal & uneven

Causes of Water Scarcity

  • Growing population — more demand for drinking, farming, industry
  • Over-irrigation — water-intensive crops, free/subsidised power
  • Industrialisation — huge water & energy needs
  • Urbanisation — housing colonies with private pumps
  • Falling water table — over-pumping of groundwater
  • Water pollution — sewage, effluents make water unusable
Scarcity is mostly human-made — can occur even in water-rich areas due to unequal access & bad management.

Multi-Purpose River Projects

  • A dam = barrier across river creating a reservoir
  • Serve many purposes at once (multi-purpose)
  • Nehru called them "temples of modern India"
  • Purposes: irrigation, hydel power, water supply, flood control, navigation, fishery, recreation
  • Integrated water-resource management

Problems with Dams

  • Regulate natural flow — harms aquatic life migration/breeding
  • Sedimentation — silt reduces reservoir capacity
  • Induced floods — sudden water release, sediment build-up
  • Displacement — submerges land/forest, uproots communities
  • Social inequality — benefits reach big farmers/industry
  • Land degradation — waterlogging & soil salinity
Narmada Bachao Andolan (Medha Patkar) opposed Sardar Sarovar Dam.

Conservation & Management

  • Rainwater harvesting — recharge groundwater, store rain
  • Recycle & reuse — treated wastewater for industry/gardens
  • Efficient irrigation — drip & sprinkler systems
  • Prevent wastage — fix leaks, water-saving habits
  • Prevent pollution — treat sewage & effluents
  • Revive tradition — tanks, johads, watershed development

Important Exam Points — Quick Recall

  • 96.5% — share of world water in oceans (saline)
  • 2.5% — share of world's freshwater
  • "Temples of modern India" — Nehru's term for multi-purpose dams
  • Bhakra-Nangal — on Sutlej; Hirakud — on Mahanadi (world's longest dam)
  • Sardar Sarovar — on Narmada; opposed by Narmada Bachao Andolan

  • Bamboo drip irrigation — Meghalaya's traditional system
  • Gendathur — Karnataka village famous for rooftop harvesting
  • Tamil Nadu — first state to make rooftop rainwater harvesting compulsory
