Minerals and Energy Resources · Geography Ch.5 · Quick Reference

Mineral Basics

  • A mineral = naturally occurring, homogenous substance with a definable internal structure
  • Ore = mineral mixed with other elements; metal can be profitably extracted
  • Rocks are combinations of minerals
  • Extracted by mining, drilling, quarrying
Over 2000 minerals identified, but only a few are abundant in rocks.

Modes of Occurrence

Ferrous Minerals

  • Contain iron; ~3/4 of value of metallic mineral production
  • Magnetite — finest ore, ~70% iron, magnetic
  • Haematite — most important industrial ore, 50–60% iron
  • Manganese — for steel & ferro-manganese (~10 kg per tonne of steel); Odisha largest producer
4 belts: Odisha-Jharkhand, Durg-Bastar-Chandrapur, Ballari-Chitradurga (Kudremukh), Maharashtra-Goa.

Mining hazards: pulmonary diseases, collapse/flooding, water pollution — a "killer industry".

Conventional Energy — Fuels

  • Coal — most abundant fossil fuel; Gondwana (Damodar valley) & Tertiary (NE)
  • Grades: Peat < Lignite < Bituminous < Anthracite (carbon rises)
  • Petroleum ("black gold") — Mumbai High, Gujarat, Assam (Digboi)
  • Natural gas — clean fuel; Krishna-Godavari basin
  • Found in anticlines & fault traps

Electricity

  • Hydro-electricity — from running water (renewable)
  • Thermal — burning coal/petroleum/gas (non-renewable); 310+ plants
  • Nuclear/Atomic — uranium & thorium; thorium in Kerala monazite sands

Conservation

  • Minerals & fossil fuels are non-renewable — form over millions of years
  • Use in a planned, sustainable way
  • Recycle metals (scrap)
  • Use substitutes & improved tech for low-grade ore
  • Save energy; shift to renewables

Important Exam Points — Quick Recall

  • Magnetite — finest iron ore (~70% iron), magnetic | Haematite — most used industrially
  • Bailadila (Chhattisgarh) & Kudremukh (Karnataka) — famous iron-ore mines
  • Bauxite — ore of aluminium; Odisha largest producer
  • Khetri (Rajasthan) — copper mines
  • Digboi (Assam) — oldest oil field | Mumbai High — offshore oil
  • Petroleum = "black gold" | Coal grades: Peat → Lignite → Bituminous → Anthracite
  • Nagarcoil–Madurai (Tamil Nadu) — largest wind farm
  • Thorium — from monazite sands of Kerala (nuclear)
  • Gobar gas = biogas from cattle dung & farm waste
  • Manganese — ~10 kg to make 1 tonne of steel
