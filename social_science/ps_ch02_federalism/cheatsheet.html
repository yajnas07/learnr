Federalism

Cheat Sheet · CBSE Class X · Civics Chapter 2

📜 What is Federalism?

A system of government where power is divided between a central authority and constituent units (states/provinces).

Two or more levels of government exist
Each level has its own jurisdiction
Powers specified in writing in Constitution
Cannot be changed unilaterally by one level
Independent judiciary resolves disputes
Each tier has own revenue sources

📌 India vs USA

USA: residuary → states (states stronger). India: residuary → Union (Centre-leaning). This makes India quasi-federal.

🇮🇳 India"Holding Together" type. Centre is dominant. J&K (until 2019) had special status. UTs directly under Centre. Hence India is quasi-federal (mix of federal + unitary).
🏛️ Why India is Quasi-Federal

India has UNITARY features mixed with federal:

Single Constitution for whole country
Single citizenship (no state citizenship)
Centre can change state boundaries
Governor appointed by President (not elected)
Article 356 — President's Rule on states
Residuary powers with Centre
All-India Services (IAS, IPS) common to Centre & States
🗣️ Linguistic States & Language Policy
📅 1956 — States ReorganisationStates redrawn on linguistic basis. Initially feared to cause disintegration, but actually strengthened unity.
🚫 No National LanguageIndia has NO national language. Hindi is OFFICIAL (not national). 22 Scheduled Languages recognised by Constitution.
📖 Three Language FormulaSchools teach 3 languages: Mother tongue + Hindi + English (or another modern Indian language).
⚠️ Common Mistake

Hindi is OFFICIAL language of Union, NOT national language. English is associate official language.

📜 Centre-State Relations Over Time
1950–1989: Congress dominance. Article 356 misused to dismiss state govts.
1989+: Coalition era begins. Regional parties become powerful. Centre forced to respect states.
1992: 73rd & 74th Amendments → Local govt becomes constitutional.
1994: SR Bommai case → SC restricts misuse of Article 356.
2000s+: "Cooperative federalism" — GST Council, NITI Aayog.

🧠 Memory Tricks
📋 List counts: "97-66-47"

Union 97, State 66, Concurrent 47. Decreasing order.

🏛️ "TIER 3"

Top (Union) · Intermediate (State) · Earth-level (Local) — 3 tiers in India.

🤝 "Holding India"

India = Holding Together. USA = Coming Together. Easy clue: India was 1 country first, USA was 13 colonies first.

📅 "73-74 in 92"

73rd = Rural (Panchayats), 74th = Urban (Municipalities). Both passed in 1992.

⚖️ "Hindi is OFFICIAL not NATIONAL"

India has NO national language. Hindi is OFFICIAL language. Don't confuse the two!
