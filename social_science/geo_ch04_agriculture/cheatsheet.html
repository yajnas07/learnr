Agriculture · Geography Ch.4 · Quick Reference

Types of Farming

  • Primitive Subsistence — slash & burn, primitive tools, monsoon-dependent
  • Intensive Subsistence — labour-intensive, high inputs, small plots
  • Commercial Farming — HYV seeds, fertilisers, crops for market
  • Plantation — single crop on large area (tea, coffee, rubber)
Slash & burn names: Jhumming (NE India), Milpa (Mexico), Roca (Brazil), Ladang (Malaysia).

Winter western disturbances help Rabi crops in NW India. Sugarcane takes almost a year.

Major Food Crops

  • Rice — kharif; needs >25°C, high humidity, >100 cm rain; staple food
  • Wheat — rabi; cool growing season, 50–75 cm rain; NW plains
  • Millets — jowar, bajra, ragi (coarse grains); ragi rich in iron & calcium
  • Maize — both kharif & rabi; needs old alluvial soil
  • Pulses — India = largest producer & consumer; fix nitrogen in soil

Reforms & Technology

  • Green Revolution — HYV seeds, irrigation, fertilisers (1960s)
  • White Revolution (Operation Flood) — boosted milk production
  • Land reforms — abolition of zamindari, "land to the tiller"
  • Collectivisation & consolidation of holdings
  • KCC (Kissan Credit Card), PAIS, crop insurance, MSP

Globalisation

  • Indian spices, muslin & cotton textiles traded worldwide since ancient times
  • British exported cotton/indigo as raw material — ruined farmers
  • Champaran (1917) — Gandhiji vs forced indigo cultivation
  • Today: competition from subsidised farm goods of developed nations
  • Shift to horticulture (fruits, vegetables, flowers), organic farming

Food Security & Future

  • Agriculture supports ~2/3 of India's population
  • Share in GDP declining, but livelihood role remains huge
  • Bhoodan–Gramdan — Vinoba Bhave's "blood-less revolution"
  • Organic farming — reduces chemicals, protects soil & health
  • Need sustainable, profitable farming for the future

Important Exam Points — Quick Recall

  • Jhumming — slash & burn farming in NE India
  • Rabi — wheat, gram, mustard | Kharif — rice, cotton, jute | Zaid — summer crops
  • Rice — staple; India 2nd largest producer after China
  • Cotton — needs black soil & 210 frost-free days; kharif crop
  • Jute — "golden fibre" of West Bengal
  • India — largest producer & consumer of pulses
  • Green Revolution — HYV seeds made India self-sufficient in food
  • Operation Flood = White Revolution (milk)
  • Bhoodan–Gramdan — led by Vinoba Bhave; began at Pochampalli
  • Champaran, 1917 — Gandhiji's movement against forced indigo cultivation
