Probability — Cheat Sheet

Class X · Mathematics Ch.14 · 8 Panels · Quick Reference

🎲 Basic Terms
Probability = a measure of chance
Always 0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1
Vocabulary
TermMeaning
Experimentaction with uncertain result
Outcomeone possible result
Eventset of favourable outcomes
Trialone performance of the experiment
Elementary event: an event with exactly one outcome.
➗ Theoretical Probability
P(E) = (favourable outcomes) ÷ (total outcomes)
Condition: all outcomes must be equally likely (fair coin / unbiased die).
Sum rule: the probabilities of all elementary events add up to 1.
Never give a probability greater than 1 or less than 0 — recheck your counting.

🎯 Two-Dice Sums
SumWaysProbability

766/36 = 1/6
Sums are NOT equally likely! 7 is the most probable sum; 2 and 12 the least.
Always count the 36 ordered pairs, e.g. (2,5) and (5,2) are different.
🧠 Exam Quick Recall
Method: (1) count total outcomes, (2) count favourable, (3) divide, (4) simplify the fraction.
Common mistakes: forgetting a card is removed (without replacement); assuming dice sums are equally likely; miscounting face cards (12, not 16).
Always simplify: 26/52 = 1/2. Leave the answer as a reduced fraction unless told otherwise.
Sanity check: a probability must land between 0 and 1.
