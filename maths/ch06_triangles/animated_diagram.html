📐
AA (or AAA)
∠A=∠P, ∠B=∠Q
Two pairs of equal angles → similar (third angle follows).
📏
SSS
AB/PQ = BC/QR = CA/RP
All three side-pairs in the same ratio → similar.
🔗
SAS
∠A=∠P & AB/PQ=AC/PR
Equal angle with proportional including sides → similar.

💡 Click any real-life situation to see how triangles solve it.
🌳Heights & Shadows
Pattern: A short object and a tall object cast shadows at the same time → similar triangles.
height / shadow = constant Example: 6 m pole → 4 m shadow. Tower → 28 m shadow.
6/4 = h/28 → h = 42 m
🪜Ladder against a Wall
Pattern: Ladder, wall and ground form a right triangle.
ladder² = height² + base² Example: 10 m ladder reaches 8 m high.
base = √(10²−8²) = 6 m
🌉Width of a River
Pattern: Construct a triangle on the bank similar to one spanning the river.
unknown width = known side × ratio Example: Similar triangles with ratio 1:3 and near side 15 m → width = 45 m.
🗼Distance Between Pole Tops
Pattern: Height difference & horizontal gap form a right triangle.
d = √(gap² + (h₂−h₁)²) Example: Poles 6 m & 11 m, 12 m apart.
d = √(12² + 5²) = 13 m
🗺️Maps & Scale Drawings
Pattern: A map is similar to the real region.
actual = map distance × scale factor Example: 1 cm on map = 50 km. 6.5 cm → 325 km.
📐Diagonal of a Rectangle
Pattern: The diagonal splits a rectangle into two right triangles.
diagonal² = length² + breadth² Example: 12 cm × 5 cm rectangle.
diagonal = √(144+25) = 13 cm
