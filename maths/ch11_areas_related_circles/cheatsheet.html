⭕ Circle Basics & π
Radius = r  |  Diameter d = 2r
π = circumference ÷ diameter

π is irrational ≈ 3.14159… — always keep it as a factor until the last step.
📏 Circumference & Arc
Circumference C = 2πr = πd
Arc length ℓ = (θ/360) × 2πr
Perimeter of semicircle = πr + 2r
Perimeter of quadrant = (πr/2) + 2r
Wheel problems: distance in n turns = n × 2πr. Turns = distance ÷ (2πr).
Perimeter ≠ area. Perimeter includes the straight edges (radii/diameter), not just the arc.
🟣 Area of a Circle
Circle A = πr²
Semicircle = ½πr²   Quadrant = ¼πr²
Circular Ring (Annulus)
A = π(R² − r²) = π(R+r)(R−r)
(R = outer radius, r = inner radius)
If diameter given: find r = d/2 BEFORE squaring. A common exam slip.
🍕 Sector of a Circle
Area of sector = (θ/360) × πr²
Arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr
Also: Area = ½ × ℓ × r
Perimeter of a sector = arc length + 2r (two radii).
Major sector = πr² − minor sector. Use 360° − θ for the major angle.
Quadrant is a sector with θ = 90°.

🧠 Exam Quick Recall
Semicircle perimeter = πr + 2r (don't forget the diameter!).
Sector perimeter = arc + 2r (don't forget the two radii!).
Common mistakes: using d instead of r; forgetting straight edges in perimeter; mixing π = 22/7 with a non-multiple of 7.
Units: length in cm, area in cm². Keep units consistent throughout.
