Distance Formula: PQ = √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²]. The dashed legs form a right triangle — the distance is its hypotenuse (Pythagoras).
Section Formula: the point P divides AB internally in the ratio m : n. Change m and n to slide P along the segment.
Drag either endpoint
Midpoint: M = ( (x₁+x₂)/2 , (y₁+y₂)/2 ) — simply the average of the coordinates. It always sits exactly halfway.
Drag any of the three vertices
Area = ½|x₁(y₂−y₃)+x₂(y₃−y₁)+x₃(y₁−y₂)|. Line the vertices up and watch the area shrink to 0 — that is the collinearity test!

🧭 Navigation & Maps

GPS positions are coordinates. The distance formula gives the straight-line distance between two locations.

🏗️ Design & Graphics

Every pixel on a screen has (x, y) coordinates. Midpoints and sections place objects precisely.

📐 Surveying Land

The area formula finds a plot's area directly from the coordinates of its corners.

🤖 Robotics

Robot arms plan paths by dividing segments in ratios — exactly the section formula.

🎮 Game Physics

Collision checks use distances; sprite centres use midpoints and centroids.

✈️ Air Traffic

Checking if three waypoints are collinear (area = 0) keeps aircraft on a straight route.
